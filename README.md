# 🏰 Ian's 80s & 90s D&D and Tabletop RPG Book Shrine (Est. 1996) 🏰

A love letter to the Web 1.0 era of **GeoCities**, **Angelfire**, and classic tabletop roleplaying games from the 1980s and 1990s.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start
- **Public 90s Shrine:** Double-click **`index.html`** or **`OPEN_RETRO_SHRINE.bat`** to view your public GeoCities/Angelfire book archive!
- **Collection Admin Database:** Double-click **`admin.html`** or **`OPEN_ADMIN_DATABASE.bat`** (or click the *"[🔑 DM Collection Database]"* link in the shrine) to launch the password-protected **Microsoft Access 97 / Windows 95** management tool!

- **Zero build steps, zero node/npm dependencies, zero external assets.** Runs 100% locally and offline in any modern web browser.

---

## 🗃️ Backend & Collection Admin Interface (Password Protected)

* **Master-Detail Navigation:** Browse, search, and jump across your entire collection with live thumbnails and record counters (`Record: 1 of 19`).
* **Four Dedicated Form Tabs:**
  1. **Book Info & System:** Title, Game System/Ruleset, Category filter, Year Published, Publisher, Cover Artist, Rarity (1–5 Stars), and Badge text (*"HOLY GRAIL"*, *"1ST PRINT"*).
  2. **Collection Log & Dates:** Acquisition Date (calendar picker), Last Played / Read Date, Condition Grade dropdown (*Mint in Shrink*, *Near Mint*, *Very Good*, *Good*, *Fair*, *Poor*), Shelf Location in your vault, Purchase Price ($), Estimated Market Value ($), and Detailed Condition Notes.
  3. **Lore & Rules:** Detailed descriptions, personal nostalgia, memorable rules/tables (THAC0 charts, potion miscibility, combat crunches), favorite quotes, and archive tags.
  4. **Cover Image & Media:** 
     - **Upload Image from Computer:** Direct local file picker (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP). Files are converted to data URLs and stored directly in your local browser database.
     - **Image URL:** Paste web image links.
     - **Custom SVG Generator:** Generate retro color-themed SVG covers on the fly with custom primary & accent colors.
* **Live GeoCities Card Preview:** See in real time exactly how the book card will appear on the public shrine as you type and upload covers!
* **Persistence & Portability:**
  - Saves instantly to browser `localStorage` and updates both the public shrine and admin console synchronously.
  - **📁 Export JSON Backup:** One-click download of your entire collection database as a `.json` backup.
  - **📥 Import JSON Backup:** Restore or merge collection backups anytime.
  - **📜 Download updated `books-data.js`:** Generates and downloads a clean, formatted `books-data.js` file that you can drop straight into the project folder to permanently bake your custom books into the repository codebase.
  - **↩ Reset Defaults:** Restores the original 19 curated 1980s & 1990s TSR and classic RPG tomes if needed.

## 📜 Cataloged 1980s & 1990s TTRPG Books
The compendium includes **19 classic tomes** complete with retro cover art representations, collector condition ratings, shelf locations, historical lore, signature mechanics, and quotes:

### 1. Advanced Dungeons & Dragons (1st Edition - TSR)
1. **Player's Handbook (1978/1980)** - David A. Trampier's iconic demon idol cover, psionics, and Bard progression.
2. **Dungeon Masters Guide (1979)** - Sutherland Efreeti cover, Appendix N, Harlot table, potion miscibility.
3. **Monster Manual (1977)** - The first AD&D hardcover ever released (Demogorgon, Beholder, Carrion Crawler).
4. **Deities & Demigods (1980 1st Printing 144-Page)** - The holy grail with the original Cthulhu and Melnibonéan mythos.
5. **Fiend Folio (1981)** - TSR UK import featuring the Githyanki, Githzerai, Death Knight, and Flumph.

### 2. D&D Basic / BECMI
6. **D&D Basic Set ("The Red Box") (1983)** - Larry Elmore's legendary dragon cover, Aleena the Cleric, and Bargle.
7. **D&D Rules Cyclopedia (1991)** - Aaron Allston's 496-page BECMI all-in-one masterpiece.

### 3. AD&D 2nd Edition & The 90s Golden Era of Campaign Settings
8. **AD&D 2nd Edition Player's Handbook (1989)** - Jeff Easley cover, THAC0 mainstreamed, priest spheres.
9. **Planescape Campaign Setting (1994 Boxed Set)** - Tony DiTerlizzi art, Zeb Cook, Sigil the Cage, Lady of Pain.
10. **Dark Sun Campaign Setting (1991 Boxed Set)** - Gerald Brom oil art, Athas, defilers, cannibal halflings.
11. **Ravenloft: Realm of Terror ("The Black Box") (1990)** - Strahd von Zarovich, Fear/Horror checks, Tarokka deck.
12. **Spelljammer: Adventures in Space (1989 Boxed Set)** - Giant Space Hamsters, Neogi, flying galleons.

### 4. Non-TSR 1980s & 1990s Tabletop Legends
13. **Call of Cthulhu (5th Edition, 1992)** - Chaosium, Sandy Petersen, 1d100 Sanity points, 1920s cosmic horror.
14. **Cyberpunk 2020 (1990)** - R. Talsorian Games, Mike Pondsmith, Friday Night Firefight, Night City chrome.
15. **Shadowrun (2nd Edition, 1992)** - FASA, Seattle 2053, magic meets machine, d6 dice pool damage staging.
16. **Vampire: The Masquerade (2nd Edition, 1992)** - White Wolf, green marble cover, Camarilla clans, Humanity.
17. **Rifts (1990)** - Palladium Books, Kevin Siembieda, Mega-Damage (M.D.C.), Glitter Boys, dimensional rifts.
18. **Paranoia (2nd Edition, 1987)** - West End Games, Friend Computer, mandatory happiness, Red clearance.
19. **Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay (1st Edition, 1986)** - Games Workshop, grim & perilous Old World, Ratcatchers, critical hit tables.

## 🕹️ Interactive Features & Audio
- **Web Audio MIDI Synthesizer:** Built-in FM-synth chiptune BGM player! Plays authentic 90s dungeon tunes (*"Crypt of the Red Dragon"*, *"Tavern of the Flumph"*, *"Underdark Wandering"*). Complete with Play/Pause, Stop, Next Track, and volume controls.
- **Polyhedral Dice Roller:** Roll `d4`, `d6`, `d8`, `d10`, `d12`, `d20`, `d100` with real click/tumbling sounds. Rolling a **Natural 20** triggers a triumphant 8-bit fanfare; rolling a **Natural 1** triggers a blunder sound!
- **3d6 In Order Forge:** Roll classic 6 abilities (`STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA`) and receive an AD&D class recommendation.
- **THAC0 Calculator:** Real-time formula calculator: `Roll Needed on 1d20 = THAC0 - Target AC`.
- **Windows 95 Tome Inspector:** Click any book card to open an authentic Windows 95 modal dialog with tabbed browsing (*General*, *Lore & Impact*, *Rules & Stat Crunch*).
- **Interactive Cyber-Guestbook:** Pre-seeded with 5 classic 90s comments (*Elminster_Shadowdale*, *Drizzt_Fan_97*, *Friend_Computer*), with a form that saves new entries directly to browser `localStorage`.
- **Easter Eggs:**
  - **Konami Code:** Press `↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A` on your keyboard to trigger **GOD MODE** with screen shake and fanfare!
  - Click the **Under Construction** sign or the **Flaming Skull** for retro DM warnings.
